From juggling apps to one screen that shows what Lena owes

Future-state journey for Riverty’s first consumer card in Germany. Value proposition: pay later anywhere, your way. Give each purchase its own due date, or bundle it into one monthly bill. You always see what you owe.

Who

Lena, 32. Heavy pay-later user, shops on her phone, two or three pay-later apps, middling salary with little cushion.

Goal

Always know exactly what she owes and when it’s due, in one place, so payday never brings surprises.

Today’s friction

Card balance and pay-later orders on two screens, two reminders in one week for different amounts. (N-02, N-07, T-103, T-104)

3 competitive advantages over the manual workaround

  1. One true total, not mental maths → fewer mistakes and missed payments.
  2. Reminders that match reality → no double-checking, no “which is right?” support calls.
  3. Control at the moment of purchase → she sets the timing, not a fixed schedule.

Two honest notes

It only covers Riverty spending. Klarna and PayPal orders stay separate, so the benefit grows as Lena moves spending onto the card. That’s the business win, and a big assumption to test.

The choice must be quick. Picking a due date or the monthly bill has to take one tap, or the flexibility brings the confusion back.
