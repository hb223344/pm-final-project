## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** Lena, 32, a heavy pay-later user who shops mostly on her phone, has two or three pay-later apps, and lives on a middling salary with little cushion.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** The share of heavy users still buying in the month after their first bill. The baseline is about 64–69%, and the target is +5 points.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** She can’t see her card balance and her pay-later orders in one place, and gets two reminders in the same week for different amounts, so she can’t tell what she really owes. (N-07, T-103, T-104)
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Partner merchant share of card volume must stay at or above 63.1%.
