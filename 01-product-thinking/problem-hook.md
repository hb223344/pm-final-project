# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which Riverty business-unit case are you using?:** BU Pay & Credit: Riverty's First Consumer Card
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Efforts and funds lost, very hard to pickup after momentum is lost.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Emailing and calling, tracking down (maybe spreadsheet) status of payments, fees etc. or just go to competitor
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must stop Riverty from being just one more swappable option at checkout, by fixing consumer's stress of tracking scattered pay-later bills and not being able to pay later in store.
- **Value proposition, based on the initiative in your assigned Riverty case, what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Pay later anywhere, your way. Give each purchase its own due date, or bundle it into one monthly bill. You always see what you owe.
